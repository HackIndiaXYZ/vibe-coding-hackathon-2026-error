Build a complete startup-quality clinic queue management platform called **QueueCare AI** using the exact visual language of a modern editorial SaaS product.

The design should feel like a premium software company, not a hospital management system.

Use a typography-first approach with oversized headlines, spacious layouts, soft mint paper backgrounds, rounded pill controls, subtle gradients, and almost no shadows.

Think:

* Modern editorial software
* Calm and premium
* Minimal but sophisticated
* Product-led design
* Beautiful enough for investors and hackathon judges

---

# DESIGN SYSTEM

## Theme

Light theme only.

Page background:
Bone Mist (#f0f7f6)

Primary text:
Bark (#17150e)

Cards:
Pure white (#ffffff)

Dividers:
Ash (#e2e2e2)

Accent:
Chartreuse (#cccc25)

Never use bright healthcare blues.

Avoid typical hospital visuals.

---

# TYPOGRAPHY

Use typography as the primary design element.

Headlines:
Huge editorial headlines.

Examples:

72px display headlines

48px section headlines

32px card titles

Use tight letter spacing.

Body text:
16px–18px.

Large amounts of whitespace.

Every section should feel like a modern magazine layout.

---

# BUTTON SYSTEM

All buttons must be fully pill-shaped.

Primary CTA:
Dark Bark background.

Secondary CTA:
Chartreuse background.

No square buttons.

No shadows.

# DESIGN RULES

Use oversized typography before adding more UI.

Prefer whitespace over borders.

Prefer gradients over shadows.

Use pill-shaped controls everywhere.

Use chartreuse sparingly as a premium accent.

Keep interfaces calm, elegant, and editorial.

The final result should look like a venture-backed healthcare startup rather than a traditional clinic management dashboard.
